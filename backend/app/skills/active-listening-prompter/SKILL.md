            Suggests clarifying questions to ask based on what someone just told you.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify what the other person actually said, separate from what might be assumed.
2. Suggest a clarifying question that checks understanding rather than shifts topic.
3. Suggest a reflective statement that shows what was heard ('sounds like...').
4. Keep suggestions short and natural to say out loud.

            ## Example

            They said: 'This project keeps changing scope on me.' -> 'When you say it keeps changing, do you mean requirements or deadline?'

            ## Tool

            This skill has no code implementation yet. When an agent has it enabled, the instructions above are injected into the model's context as guidance to follow.
