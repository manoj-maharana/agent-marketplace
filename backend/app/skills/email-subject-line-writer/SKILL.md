            Suggests clear, specific subject lines for an email you've drafted.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify the single most important thing the recipient needs to know or do.
2. Lead the subject line with that, not with a vague topic label.
3. Keep it short enough to not get cut off (roughly under 60 characters).
4. Offer 2-3 variants if the tone (formal vs. casual) isn't specified.

            ## Example

            Email asking to reschedule a meeting -> 'Reschedule our 2pm to Thursday?'

            ## Tool

            This skill has no code implementation yet. When an agent has it enabled, the instructions above are injected into the model's context as guidance to follow.
