            Turns a routine into a simple daily checklist you can track.

            ## Instructions

            1. Take the routine described and split it into its individual habits.
2. Phrase each item as a short, checkable action ("Drink water", not "Be healthy").
3. Order them the way they'd naturally happen in a day.
4. Keep the list under ~7 items so it stays usable.

            ## Example

            Morning routine -> Stretch 5 min / Drink a glass of water / Review today's top 3 tasks.
