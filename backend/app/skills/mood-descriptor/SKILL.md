            Turns a vague vibe into concrete visual and sensory descriptors.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify the vague vibe or feeling given.
2. Translate it into concrete sensory details: light, color, sound, texture.
3. Keep details consistent with one coherent mood rather than mixing contradictory ones.
4. Output as a short descriptive passage, not a bullet list of adjectives.

            ## Example

            'cozy but a little sad' -> 'a dim lamp against rain-streaked glass, a half-finished cup of tea gone cold.'

            ## Tool

            This skill has no code implementation yet. When an agent has it enabled, the instructions above are injected into the model's context as guidance to follow.
