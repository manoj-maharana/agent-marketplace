            Suggests natural conversation starters for a given setting or relationship.

            ## Instructions

            1. Consider the setting (work, casual, formal) and relationship given.
2. Suggest an open-ended question rather than a yes/no one.
3. Keep it light and low-stakes - avoid anything overly personal for the relationship level.
4. Offer 2-3 options in case the first doesn't fit.

            ## Example

            Setting: work conference, stranger at a booth -> 'What's been the most interesting thing you've seen here so far?'
