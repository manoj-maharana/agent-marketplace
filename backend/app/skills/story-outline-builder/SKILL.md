            Structures a premise into a three-act outline with beats.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify the premise's central conflict.
2. Structure into three acts: setup, rising complication, resolution.
3. Under each act, list 2-4 concrete beats (events), not vague themes.
4. Make sure the ending beat actually resolves the act-one conflict.

            ## Example

            Premise: a detective doubts their own memory -> Act 1: case begins, Act 2: evidence contradicts memory, Act 3: truth revealed.
