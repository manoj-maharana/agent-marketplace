            Flags common methodology red flags described in a study summary.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify the study's sample size, control group (if any), and measurement method from the summary given.
2. Check for common red flags: no control group, tiny sample, self-reported data treated as objective, correlation framed as causation.
3. Note which red flags actually apply here - don't list ones that don't.
4. Keep the tone as 'worth double-checking,' not a verdict, since only a summary was reviewed.

            ## Example

            Study of 12 participants, no control group -> flag: sample too small to generalize, no baseline to compare against.
