            Builds a study schedule across the days you have before a deadline.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify the material to cover and the deadline.
2. Divide the material into topic-sized chunks.
3. Spread chunks across the available days, leaving the last day or two for review, not new material.
4. Note which days benefit from spaced repetition of earlier topics.

            ## Example

            10 chapters, 7 days -> days 1-5: 2 chapters/day, day 6: review, day 7: practice test.

            ## Tool

            This skill has no code implementation yet. When an agent has it enabled, the instructions above are injected into the model's context as guidance to follow.
