            Rewrites a dense summary at a simpler reading level.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify the core claim(s) in the dense summary.
2. Rewrite using shorter sentences and everyday vocabulary in place of jargon.
3. Keep the meaning intact - simplifying isn't the same as omitting the point.
4. Define any term that can't be avoided, briefly, in-line.

            ## Example

            'The mitochondria is the organelle responsible for cellular respiration' -> 'Mitochondria turn food into usable energy.'
