            Helps flesh out the rules, geography, and history of a fictional world.

            ## Instructions

            1. Identify what's already established about the world.
2. Pick one unresolved area to flesh out at a time (geography, history, rules/magic system, culture).
3. Make additions consistent with what's already established - flag if a new idea would contradict it.
4. Keep answers grounded in how the world's rules would realistically play out.

            ## Example

            World has scarce water -> logically implies water rights would be a major source of political conflict.

            ## Tool

            This skill has no code implementation yet. When an agent has it enabled, the instructions above are injected into the model's context as guidance to follow.
